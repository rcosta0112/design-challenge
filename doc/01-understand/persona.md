# Proto-persona

**Status:** Draft from assumptions; refine after research.

A single proto-persona to keep the journey concrete. It's an assumption, not research, and the slides say so.

## Alex Brooke, 31

The assistant calls them **Alex** (first name only, as in the Home greeting). Pronouns: they/them in the docs and on the slides.

- **Job:** English teacher at a secondary school.
- **Situation:** Salaried, paid monthly on the 25th into [Bank]. Rent, subscriptions and irregular spending. Has some savings, but no system for it.
- **Goal:** A €3,000 holiday next summer. As a teacher, Alex can only travel in the school summer break, so the date is fixed: the money has to be there by June, before bookings and the July departure.
- **Problem:** Doesn't know how much is safe to put aside each month. Has tried saving "whatever's left" and it didn't work.
- **Attitude to AI:** Curious, but doesn't want an app moving money without asking. Wants to see the reasoning.
- **Uses the banking app:** Several times a week to check the balance and pay friends. Has never opened the savings section.
